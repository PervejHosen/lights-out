CHANGES AND ADDITIONS

- Extended the temporal coverage beyond the previously published
  2019–2024 dataset.

- Retained the 2019–2024 records from Hossain et al. (2025),
  reused under the CC BY 4.0 licence.

- Independently collected additional daily electricity records
  after December 30, 2024 from the Bangladesh Power Development
  Board (BPDB) Daily Generation Archive.

- Integrated the additional records with the existing dataset
  structure.

- Adapted the extraction procedure for selected variables affected
  by changes in the BPDB report format.

- Updated the documentation to distinguish the original 2019–2024
  records from the newly collected post-2024 records.

- Added attribution and citation information for the original dataset.
