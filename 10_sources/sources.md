## Method notes
- Gas to power conversion: 1 mmcfd is about 1,030 MMBtu a day; MW per mmcfd = 42,900 divided by the heat rate in Btu per kWh. Engineering estimate, 3.6 to 5.7 MW per mmcfd (central 4.5).
